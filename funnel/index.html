Questions, answered

What contractors ask before partnering

Is this a loan? Will I owe money?
No. There's no loan, no debt, and no repayment. This is "Funding-as-a-Service" — we invest the equipment, labor and operations into your job and earn our share only when the project is completed and paid. If the job doesn't happen, you owe nothing.
Do I lose my customer or my lead?
Never. The lead is yours and stays yours. You remain the face of the client and the only point of contact. We operate behind the scenes under your brand so your relationship is fully protected.
How much do I actually earn?
You earn roughly 25% of the project value with zero capital invested. On a $50,000 job, that's about $12,500 in your pocket for bringing the work and staying the face of the client. Exact splits are confirmed per project on your strategy call.
Which industries do you work with?
We specialize in restoration and cleaning trades: water damage restoration, mold remediation, carpet cleaning, and building/commercial cleaning. If your work is adjacent to these, apply and we'll let you know if it's a fit.
What do I need to qualify?
An active lead or project (or a steady flow of them) in one of the trades above. You don't need equipment, a crew, or cash — that's exactly what we bring. Apply in under two minutes and book a free strategy call to map your first job.
